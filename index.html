#1 Trestle Bike Park 🏆 Tier A YOUR HOME PARK
🚗 ~75 min / 67 mi 📍 Winter Park, CO 🏔️ 40+ miles of trails
💛 Lift ticket required ✅ eMTB on all lifts Open daily · Full Time June 12
▼

The benchmark for everything else on this list. Fastest-growing bike park in North America, 40+ miles of lift-served gravity trails, three lifts, and the largest rental fleet in the US. Green to double black — all styles covered. Your preferred zone is the blue flow trails: Long Trail, Shy Ann, and Green World are the sweet spot. Open daily in summer, weekends only in spring.

🚦 Access & How It Works

Lifts: Three lifts — Gemini Express (main), Panoramic Express (upper), and Iron Horse Express. Gemini gets you to the bulk of blue trails. Panoramic accesses upper expert terrain.

eMTB: eMTBs permitted on all three chairlifts and the gondola. No restrictions — Class 1 confirmed.

Uphill access: Free uphill pass available — sign waiver at Guest Services in Balcony House. Must be visible at all times on designated uphill routes.

Parking: Winter Park Resort base area. Pay parking on peak days. Arrive by 9am on weekends.

Address: 85 Parsenn Rd, Winter Park, CO 80482.

Season: Now open weekends. Full-time daily opens June 12, 2026. Closes late September / early October.

💛 Lift tickets required. Day tickets vary by date — book online in advance for best price. Season passes available. Twilight tickets (Fri/Sat 3–6:30pm) are a cheaper option. Military discount available with 48hr advance booking. Ikon Pass holders can add a Trestle season pass.
✅ eMTB fully welcome. All lifts, all trails. No restrictions on your Copperhead. Best use: ride up in Eco/eMTB mode to explore, save battery for the shuttle back up on tougher climbs.

#2 Keystone Bike Park 🏆 Tier A
🚗 ~85 min / 75 mi 📍 Keystone, CO 🏔️ 55+ miles of trails
💛 Lift ticket required ✅ eMTB allowed on ski area trails Summer season
▼

One of the most raw and rugged lift-accessed parks in the US. 55+ miles of trails — heavy on technical, progressive terrain with rock gardens, drops, wooden features, and long sustained descents. More technical overall than Trestle — the greens here ride like blues. Rewarding for intermediate-to-advanced riders who want long, committing runs. Drop Zone is legendary.

🚦 Access & How It Works

Lifts: Gondola (Argentina) from base to mid-mountain. River Run Gondola for main access. Lifts load/unload bikes for you — staff handles it.

eMTB: eMTBs permitted on ski area trails per Summit County policy. Confirmed for Keystone resort-managed terrain.

Parking: River Run Village base area. Free parking available in satellite lots with shuttle to base.

Address: 22010 US-6, Keystone, CO 80435.

Season: Summer — check keystoneresort.com for 2026 opening date (typically late June).

Note: Early season = gondola to mid-mountain only (~1/3 of trails open). Full mountain opens once snow clears upper terrain.

💛 Lift tickets required. Purchase at keystoneresort.com or on-site. Epic Pass holders check bike park access. Tickets typically cheaper than Trestle.
✅ eMTB confirmed on ski area trails. Summit County policy explicitly permits eMTBs on resort-managed terrain at Keystone, Breckenridge, and Copper Mountain.

#3 Granby Ranch Bike Park ⚡ Tier B
🚗 ~80 min / 75 mi 📍 Granby, CO 🏔️ 40+ mi XC · DH park
💛 Bike Haul day ticket ✅ eMTB — all XC trails Wed–Sun · Opens June 4
▼

Smaller and more relaxed than Trestle — a good add-on day when you're already in Winter Park (20 min away). 40+ miles of XC trails, all eMTB-friendly, plus a lift-served downhill park on the Quickdraw Express. Less intense crowds, relaxed vibe, great views of Grand County. The XC trail network is the real highlight here — smooth berms, flow sections, and a great backdrop.

🚦 Access & How It Works

Lift: Quickdraw Express chairlift. Operates Wed–Sun, 9:30am–5pm. Opening Day June 4, 2026.

eMTB: All 40+ miles of XC trails are explicitly eMTB-friendly per Granby Ranch. Lift-served downhill trails — check on-site for current eMTB lift policy.

Tickets: "Bike Haul Day Ticket" for lift + all trails. "Trail Access Day Ticket" for trails only (no lift). Season passes also available.

Address: 1000 Village Rd, Granby, CO 80446.

Combo tip: 20 minutes from Trestle — combine both parks for a two-day trip or morning/afternoon split.

✅ All XC trails eMTB-friendly confirmed. 55 total eMTB-designated trails per Trailforks. Lift-served DH — verify current eMTB lift policy on-site when you arrive.
💛 Bike Haul Day Ticket required for lift access. Trail-only access is a separate (cheaper) ticket. Check granbyranch.com for current pricing.

#4 Breckenridge Bike Park ⚡ Tier B
🚗 ~100 min / 85 mi 📍 Breckenridge, CO 🏔️ Peak 8 · Colorado SuperChair
💛 Lift ticket required ✅ eMTB on ski area trails More relaxed vibe
▼

Breckenridge is known more for its XC town trails ("Breck is one big trailhead") than its bike park, but the lift-served terrain on Peak 8 via the Colorado SuperChair is solid fun. More relaxed and less intense than Trestle or Keystone. Can get very crowded due to tourist traffic at the base. Better for a half-day or as part of a Breck town trip.

🚦 Access & How It Works

Lift: Colorado SuperChair at base of Peak 8. Single chairlift accessing lift-served bike park terrain.

eMTB: eMTBs permitted on ski area trails per Summit County / resort policy. Confirmed for Breckenridge resort-managed terrain.

Crowd warning: Breckenridge base area is a major tourist destination — crowds at the base can be significant on weekends. All lifts start from the base.

Address: 1599 Ski Hill Rd, Breckenridge, CO 80424.

Season: Check breckenridge.com for 2026 bike park opening date.

✅ eMTB confirmed on ski area trails. Summit County policy explicitly permits eMTBs on resort terrain. Town trails (Middle Flume, XC network) — verify eMTB access separately as some are USFS-managed.
📍 Town trail network is the real draw. The lift-served park is smaller than Trestle or Keystone, but Breck's town-based XC trails (Middle Flume, etc.) are excellent if you want a mixed day.

#5 Copper Mountain Bike Park ⚡ Tier B
🚗 ~95 min / 80 mi 📍 Copper Mountain, CO 🏔️ Woodward Express lift
💛 Lift ticket required ✅ eMTB on ski area trails Family-friendly vibe
▼

Copper's "low-key" bike park is served by the Woodward Express lift. Great beginner-to-intermediate experience with a relaxed family-friendly atmosphere. Not as extensive as Trestle or Keystone, but the trails are well-designed and less intimidating. Good option for a mellow day or when you want less intensity. Woodward connection adds terrain park-style features.

🚦 Access & How It Works

Lift: Woodward Express chairlift. Main access point for bike park terrain.

eMTB: eMTBs permitted on ski area trails. Summit County + resort policy confirmed for Copper Mountain.

Vibe: Family-friendly, relaxed atmosphere. Less intimidating than Keystone or Trestle for newer riders. Good for progression riding.

Address: 209 Ten Mile Circle, Copper Mountain, CO 80443.

Season: Check coppercolorado.com for 2026 bike park dates.

✅ eMTB confirmed on ski area trails. Summit County policy covers Copper Mountain resort-managed terrain.
🗺️ Key Trails
TrailDiffCharacter

#6 Vail Bike Park ⚡ Tier B
🚗 ~110 min / 100 mi 📍 Vail, CO 🏔️ 2 greens · 6 blues · 9 blacks
💛 Lift ticket required ⚠️ eMTB — verify before going Sign waiver to access
▼

Excellent bike park terrain — most of the mountain is intermediate to expert, and the trails are well-maintained and long. Two green trails, six blue, nine blacks — heavily skewed toward experienced riders. Epic atmosphere. The liability waiver gets you access to miles of epic downhill. Worth the drive for a serious all-day session. Farthest option on this list.

🚦 Access & How It Works

Access: Sign a liability waiver at Guest Services. After that, miles of downhill terrain open. No lift ticket cost noted — waiver-based access, but confirm current policy at vail.com.

eMTB: Vail's eMTB policy on lift-served terrain is not explicitly confirmed in current sources — verify directly at vail.com or call Guest Services before making the drive.

Terrain: 2 greens, 6 blues, 9 blacks. Most intermediate-to-expert. Green trails are limited — this is not a beginner park.

Address: 292 E Meadow Dr, Vail, CO 81657.

Season: Check vail.com for 2026 opening dates.

⚠️ eMTB policy unconfirmed for lift access. Verify directly with Vail resort before making the 110-minute drive. Call Guest Services or check vail.com bike park FAQ.

#7 Steamboat Bike Park ⚡ Tier B
🚗 ~160 min / 160 mi 📍 Steamboat Springs, CO 🏔️ 2,200 ft lift-served vertical
💛 Lift ticket required ⚠️ eMTB — verify before going Overnight trip recommended
▼

Steamboat has significantly expanded its downhill terrain — 2,200 vertical feet of lift-served trails now. Was once known only for XC, but the downhill park is now a legitimate destination. Rustler's Ridge (blue) has rollers, berms, and drops — classic flow trail style. At 160 minutes it's a long day-trip; better as an overnight stay. Beautiful setting.

🚦 Access & How It Works

Lift: Gondola access to bike park. 2,200 ft of lift-served vertical — the most on this list after Trestle.

eMTB: Not explicitly confirmed for lift access — verify with Steamboat resort directly before making the 2hr 40min drive.

Address: 2305 Mt Werner Circle, Steamboat Springs, CO 80487.

Season: Check steamboat.com for 2026 opening dates.

Best as: Overnight trip — pair with a night in Steamboat Springs, which has a great downtown scene.

⚠️ eMTB lift policy unconfirmed. Verify before making the 160-minute drive. Call Steamboat Guest Services or check steamboat.com.

#8 Snowmass Bike Park ⚡ Tier B
🚗 ~175 min / 170 mi 📍 Snowmass Village, CO 🏔️ Aspen area · Multi-day trip
💛 Lift ticket required ⚠️ eMTB — verify before going Multi-day trip recommended
▼

Nearly 3 hours from Westminster — this is a destination trip, not a day ride. Snowmass has excellent bike park terrain in a stunning setting. Best approached as a 2–3 day Aspen-area adventure. The mountain is massive with long sustained descents. Worth it as a trip but too far for a casual day out.

🚦 Access & How It Works

Lift: Elk Camp Gondola from Snowmass base. Main access point for bike park terrain.

eMTB: Not explicitly confirmed — verify with Snowmass resort before going.

Address: 100 Elbert Lane, Snowmass Village, CO 81615.

Best as: 2–3 day trip paired with Aspen-area riding. Combine with a night in Snowmass Village or Aspen.

Season: Check aspensnowmass.com for 2026 bike park dates.

⚠️ eMTB lift policy unconfirmed. At 175 minutes each way, verify before making the drive. Check aspensnowmass.com or call Guest Services.
